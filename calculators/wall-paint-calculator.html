Work out exactly how much paint you need — walls, doors, windows and coats all accounted for.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Add each wall — enter the length and height in centimetres for every wall you're painting. Include the ceiling too, if needed.
  2. Add doors and windows — these are deducted automatically from your total paintable area.
  3. Choose your paint type — coverage rates vary, so pick the closest match or set a custom rate.
  4. Set coats and tin size — 2 coats is recommended for most jobs.
  5. Read your results — total litres needed and tins to buy update instantly on the right.
