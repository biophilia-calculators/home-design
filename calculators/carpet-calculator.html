Fitted Carpet Calculator

Work out how much fitted carpet you need based on your room area and standard roll widths — no guesswork, no waste.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Choose your unit — Square Meters or Square Feet.
  2. Enter your room's length and width in that unit.
  3. Select your carpet roll width — most carpets come in 3.66m, 4m, or 5m rolls, or enter a custom width.
  4. Set a waste allowance to account for pattern matching, seams, and offcuts (15% is a safe default for most rooms).
  5. Read your results instantly — total room area, carpet length needed, and total carpet area including waste.
