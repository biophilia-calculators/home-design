Work out exactly how many tiles you need, with wastage built in — no more guessing at the checkout.

How to Use This Calculator

  1. Enter your room dimensions — measure the length and width of the room in centimetres.
  2. Pick your tile size — choose a standard size or select Custom to enter your own tile dimensions.
  3. Set a wastage allowance — 10% is recommended for most rooms to cover cuts and breakage.
  4. Add tiles per box (optional) — if you know this, we'll tell you exactly how many boxes to order.
  5. Read your results — the tile count, room area, and a visual preview update instantly on the right.
